Partnership & Coordination Hub – Manila

Beginning in early 2027, LIMRA Health Care plans to establish a dedicated healthcare partnership and coordination presence in Manila.

The proposed hub would provide continuity beyond individual conferences, institutional visits and introductory meetings. It would support structured follow-up, stakeholder engagement and the development of sustainable India–Philippines healthcare relationships.

The proposed Manila hub would support:
  • Philippine Hospital Engagement
  • Indian Healthcare Delegations
  • Clinical & Specialist Collaboration
  • CME & Training Programs
  • Healthcare Leadership Meetings
  • Long-Term Partnership Development
  • Institutional Coordination and Follow-Up
