Our Institutional Principles

01

Any future commercial relationship between LIMRA Health Care and a participating healthcare institution would be transparently discussed, documented and agreed upon by the concerned parties.

02

Any investment, joint venture or sensitive hospital-specific discussion would be handled confidentially and directly between the interested institutions, subject to appropriate due diligence.

03

All clinical and patient-care decisions would remain exclusively with the concerned doctors, hospitals and patients.
